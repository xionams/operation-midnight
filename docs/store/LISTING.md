Operation Midnight

Build a base, harvest the ore, and break the enemy before they break you.

## Full description

A near-future military RTS built for one hand on a phone screen.

Land on a cold battlefield with a Command HQ and two vehicles. Put up a
refinery, get harvesters onto the ore, and turn credits into an army
before the commander across the map turns theirs into one first.

**A real economy.** Credits only arrive when a harvester physically
drives to a field, loads, and drives back. Ore fields run out. The rich
ground is in the middle where both sides can reach it, so sitting at home
is a decision with a cost.

**An opponent that plays the same game you do.** The enemy commander
pays for everything it fields, scouts before it commits, gathers a force
rather than trickling units into your guns, pulls back when a push has
failed, rebuilds what you destroy, and expands when its own ore runs
thin. It is not handed free units or free money.

**Rock, paper, armour.** Rifle squads shred infantry and bounce off
tanks. Anti-armour teams do the reverse. Artillery outranges turrets but
has to stop to fire. Engineers capture buildings, spies walk in disguised,
and infantry in a building are far harder to shift than infantry in a
field.

**Three battlefields**, each with a different economy: a balanced map, one
that starves you at home and forces an early fight, and a long corridor
that lets both sides build up before they meet.

**Built for a phone.** Tap to select, hold and drag to box-select, drag
to pan, pinch to zoom. The match saves itself when you are interrupted
and picks up where it left off.

No ads. No purchases. No account. No internet permission.
